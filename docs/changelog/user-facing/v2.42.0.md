# v2.42.0 — Your documents can now follow you to a new computer

Budojo encrypts your athletes' medical certificates, and until now the key that unlocks them stayed locked to the computer that created it. That meant a backup restored on a *new* computer brought back everything — athletes, attendance, payments — but couldn't open the documents. This release fixes that.

## 🔑 Recovery keys

There's a new **Recovery keys** section at the bottom of **Data & backup**.

**Do this once, today:** click **Reveal recovery code**, copy the code, and paste it into your password manager. That single line is what lets a future you open your documents on a different computer. Treat it like a password — anyone who has it can open your documents, so don't store it inside the backup itself.

**If you ever move to a new computer:** install Budojo, restore your backup, then paste the saved code into **Restore keys from a recovery code**. Budojo restarts, and your medical certificates open normally again.

If you don't save the code, nothing changes from before: your athletes, attendance and payments still restore anywhere — only the encrypted documents stay locked to the old machine.
