# v2.48.0 — More than one price, and more than one month

## 💶 More than one monthly fee

Until now your academy had exactly one monthly fee. If you charge by how often someone trains — **2 lezioni a settimana 55 €, 3 lezioni 65 €** — there was nowhere to put that, and the only way to tell the two groups apart was to bend something else into the shape.

There is now a **price list**. In your academy settings, under the monthly fee, add as many tiers as you charge: a name you choose, the amount, and how many lessons a week it buys. Then on each athlete pick which one they are on.

- **Nothing changes until you add one.** No tiers means every athlete pays the monthly fee, exactly as before.
- **The fee follows the athlete.** Their Payments tab says which tier they are on and what it costs, and marking a month paid records that amount.
- **Changing a price never rewrites the past.** What someone already paid stays what they paid.
- **Deleting a tier does not delete the people on it.** They go back to the academy's standard fee, and the confirmation tells you how many that is before you do it.

## 📆 Trimestrale, semestrale, annuale

A quarterly payment used to mean ticking three months and pretending they were three payments. They weren't — the athlete handed you money once and has one receipt.

You can now say how often each athlete pays — **monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or annual** — and record it as **one payment covering the whole period**.

- The months table shows all three months of a quarter as paid, with **the amount on one row** and the range — *January to March 2026* — written under each of them, so a row without a figure never reads as a gap.
- Marking or undoing tells you what it will actually do: *"Undo Mario Rossi's payment covering January to March 2026?"* rather than a bare month name.
- **Undoing works from any month in the period** and removes the whole thing. One payment, one receipt.
- The unpaid list, the paid badge, the reminder email and the athlete's own reminder all understand periods — nobody on a quarterly gets chased in month two.

## 🎟️ Carnets, finished off

- **A carnet can now cover the past.** Set the date it starts covering from, and sessions already on the register inside that window count immediately — which is what you want when you sell a carnet to someone who has been training all month. Moving that date moves the expiry with it, and the app shows you where it lands before you confirm.
- **A carnet created by mistake can be deleted.** The sessions stay on the register and simply go back to being uncovered; the confirmation says how many that is.
- **The sale date and the price are on the card.** Selling a carnet *is* the payment — now it says so.
- **Carnet money is in the revenue chart.** A €70 carnet valid twelve months shows as about €5.83 a month across the months it covers, the same way a yearly fee does. The chart now means all your revenue, not just the monthly fees.

## 🖥️ On the desktop

The window buttons at the top right used to float over the page, with text sliding underneath them. There is now a proper bar for them to sit on.
