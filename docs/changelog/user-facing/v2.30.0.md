# v2.30.0 — 2026-05-23

The social-engagement train: peer presence, share-worthy belt promotions, weekly recap pushes, achievement badges, and a monthly mat-hours leaderboard. Five slices, one release.

## 🥋 "Chi viene stasera?" peer preview

Land on the "Sono qui oggi" page and see who else from your academy has already self-marked for today — up to 8 faces, an overflow chip for the rest. Privacy stays first-class: each athlete can opt out from the profile page ("Mostra agli altri quando mi alleno") and their row disappears from the preview while still counting toward the academy's own records.

## 📸 Share your belt promotion to Instagram

Belt-promotion posts in the feed get a "Condividi" button. One tap generates a story-sized image (1080×1920) right in the browser — gradient backdrop, your name, the new belt — and hands it to the native share sheet on supported devices. On a desktop or a browser without Web Share, the image downloads so you can post it manually. No server round-trip, no storage cost, zero latency.

## 📬 Sunday weekly recap

Every Sunday at 19:00 a push arrives summarising your week: training days, total mat hours (1.5 h/session), and the three peers you crossed paths with most often. Tap the push to land on a dedicated recap page that mirrors the same numbers, plus a Web Share button to push the summary out as plain text if you want bragging rights. The notification dedupes — if a worker retries, you don't get pinged twice.

## 🏆 Achievement badges

Five lifetime milestones now unlock automatically:

- 🥋 First class
- 🔥 30-day streak
- 💯 100 sessions
- 🎂 1 year at the academy
- 🎉 Belt promotion

They evaluate every time an attendance row is created, plus a nightly 02:00 cron for the time-based ones. Unlocked badges surface on your public profile (`/u/<handle>`) so the world sees what you've done, not just the belt you wear.

## 📊 Monthly mat-hours leaderboard

A new card on the stats / profile pages shows the top 5 athletes by sessions this month (drilldown via `?month=YYYY-MM` query), with hours computed at 1.5 h per session. Athletes who opt out from the profile page ("Mostra il mio nome nella classifica") still count toward the rank — their row appears as "Anonimo" with the session count visible. Your own row is highlighted when you appear in the top 5.
