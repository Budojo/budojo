# v2.13.0 — 2026-05-14

Browser push notifications land. Opt in once from your profile and Budojo dings the moment something happens — even with the app closed in another tab.

## 🔔 New: Browser push notifications

A new **Browser notifications** section on `/dashboard/profile` lets you turn on push notifications per device. Click *Enable on this device*, accept the browser prompt, and from then on Budojo will deliver a system-level push notification when:

- Someone replies to a community post you previously commented on.

Each device you opt in from is listed in the same panel; you can revoke individual devices without affecting the others (useful if you sign in from a friend's computer once and don't want to keep getting pushes there).

A few details worth knowing:

- **Works inside the installed Android app** (Play Store install or "Add to Home Screen"). Safari supports push only inside an installed PWA, on 16.4+.
- **Browser-level permission** — once you click *Block* on the prompt, the SPA can't ask again. You can re-enable in your browser's site settings.
- **No iOS push yet** for the regular browser tab on Safari < 16.4 — those users see a "not supported" notice instead of the toggle.

More notification types (belt celebrations, new academy events) follow in the next release — same opt-in surface, no additional steps from you.
