# v2.29.0 — 2026-05-22

The "Sono qui oggi" feature you've been asking about: athletes self-register their own presence — the instructor stops doing roll call by hand.

## 🥋 Athletes can self-register their presence

The 07:00 "today is training day" push now lands on a dedicated page with one big button: **Sono qui oggi**. Tap it, you're registered for tonight's class. Tap again to cancel (your own self-mark only — instructor marks stay protected).

Three on-page states keep the flow honest: today-is-training-day with the mark button, today-is-not-training-day with a quiet "enjoy the rest day" panel, and the already-marked confirmation with a cancel option when your row was self-marked.

A small "Self" pill shows next to the name on the instructor's daily check-in widget, so you can tell at a glance who registered themselves vs who you marked manually — and spot anomalies if any.

## 🧪 Under the hood

The full HTTP service layer of the SPA — `AuthService`, `CommunityService`, `AttendanceService`, and every smaller service in `core/services/` — now ships with Vitest coverage. The same goes for the remaining shared components and utility functions. No user-visible change, but a regression sieve that didn't exist a week ago.
