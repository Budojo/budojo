# v2.44.0 — Your backups finally leave this computer

## 💾 Pick a folder, and every backup goes there

A backup sitting on the same disk as your data does not survive that disk. This release closes that gap with no account to create and nothing to configure: **Data & backup → Backup folder → Choose folder**.

Point it at a folder your cloud service already syncs — OneDrive, Dropbox, iCloud Drive, the Google Drive app — or at a network drive or a USB stick. Budojo writes the file, and whatever you already run carries it off the machine. It stays off until you choose a folder, and nothing leaves this computer before you do.

Budojo only ever touches archives it created. Your own files in that folder are left completely alone.

## 🕓 Two weeks of history, not two days

Backups run every six hours, and until now only the seven most recent were kept — barely 42 hours. Fine for a mistake you notice straight away, useless for one you notice on Monday.

Now two things are kept side by side: the **six most recent** archives, whatever time they were made, plus the **last backup of each of the past fourteen days**. About two weeks to fall back on, for about the same disk space you would have spent holding two days at full density.

## ↩️ Restore works

The **Restore** button on each backup did nothing — it never even appeared. It does now, with a confirmation before anything is replaced.

- **Academy logos and athlete photos load again** instead of showing a broken image.
- **Budojo's own icon** in the Start menu, the taskbar and the installer, in place of the generic Electron one.
