# v2.47.0 — Carnets, for the ones who come and go

## 🎟️ Entry carnets

Some athletes train six or seven times a year. Charging them a month at a time is either unfair to them or lossy for you, and until now the app had no answer — so it happened off-book, in cash and in a notebook.

A **carnet** is a pre-paid pack of sessions. Set a price and a size once in your academy settings — say **€70 for 10 entries** — and a **Carnet ingressi** section appears on every athlete's Payments tab. Sell one, and from then on the app counts the entries down for you.

Each carnet carries a **four-character code** (`A7K2`) printed nowhere and stored everywhere: read it off the athlete's card and you know which carnet you are looking at. The letters and digits that get misread by hand — `0` and `O`, `1` and `I` and `L` — are simply not in the alphabet.

A carnet is **valid 12 months** from the day it was bought. You can back-date the sale, which is the point if you are transcribing a paper register.

**If you don't sell carnets, you will never see any of this.** Leave the price or the size empty and the whole concept stays out of your way.

## 🔢 The count takes care of itself

Mark someone present and, if that month is not already covered by their monthly fee, one entry comes off their carnet. Nothing to remember, nothing to tick.

The rules, in plain terms:

- **The monthly fee wins.** If the athlete has paid for that month, their carnet is not touched. It is a fallback, never a second charge.
- **The date on the session is the date that counts.** Filling in last month's sessions is normal, and each one is judged against the coverage that was in force back then — not today's.
- **Fixing a mistake costs one entry, not two.** Delete a presence and its entry comes straight back, so the usual "wrong day, delete, right day" costs exactly one session.
- **Holding two carnets spends the one expiring first**, so you lose the fewest entries to expiry.
- **Presence is never blocked.** An athlete with no monthly fee and no carnet is still marked present. The register is there to record what happened; sorting out the money is a separate conversation.

## 👀 The balance where you need it

- **On the roster**, next to the paid badge — a small ticket chip with the number of entries left. It turns amber at two or fewer, which is the moment to ask "vuoi rinnovare?" before they walk out.
- **On the athlete's Payments tab**, a card with the code, the balance and the expiry date, plus a **Registro ingressi** listing exactly which sessions the carnet paid for. Past and spent carnets stay there too.
- **In the athlete's own app**, so they stop having to ask you.
