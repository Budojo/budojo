# v2.12.0 — 2026-05-14

A short release focused on two things: a new public page that documents how to delete your Budojo account (now linked from the Play Store data-safety form), and a small polish pass on the community feed.

## 📜 New page: Account deletion

A new public page at `https://budojo.it/account-deletion` (and `/account-deletion/it` for the Italian version) explains exactly how to permanently delete your Budojo account: how to request it, what data is removed, what is retained for accounting / legal reasons, and the 30-day grace window during which you can cancel the request.

The page mirrors the layout of `/privacy` and `/sub-processors` and is not behind login — anyone can read it, including Play Store reviewers who need a public URL during policy review.

Nothing about the deletion machinery itself changed in this release: the email-request flow, 30-day grace, and the hourly purge cron have been live for several releases. The page just documents what already happens, in plain language.

## ✨ Community feed — small polish

Two small tweaks reported on the v2.11.0 Android internal-testing build:

- **More breathing room** between the RSVP buttons (Going / Maybe) and between the reaction buttons (👏 / 🙏) on phone widths. They felt cramped before; now they sit on the same 8-pixel grid as the rest of the design.
- **Light skin-tone modifier** on the reaction emojis — 👏🏻 and 🙏🏻 — so they render consistently across iOS, Android, and the in-app WebView instead of falling back to the platform-default yellow.
