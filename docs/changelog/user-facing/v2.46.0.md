# v2.46.0 — Faces on the roster

## 📸 Athletes can have a photo

Open an athlete and you will find a **Photo** card: upload one, replace it, remove it. It shows up on the roster too, so with sixty white belts you can find the right row by looking instead of reading.

A photo is 2 MB at most, in PNG, JPG or WebP.

**Why not "import from WhatsApp"?** It was the first thing asked for, and it cannot be built: neither WhatsApp nor Instagram lets an app read someone else's profile picture — Instagram closed the last interface that could in December 2024. And taking a person's photo from another platform into your gym's records is not yours to do. The photo has to be given to you, which is what this card is for.

## ✅ Attendance is one click away

**Presenze** now sits in the sidebar, next to Athletes. It was only ever reachable behind the **+ Create** button — fine for taking attendance, wrong for looking at it, since consulting who was there yesterday is not creating anything.

## 👤 Your avatar opens your profile

Clicking your name and picture at the bottom of the sidebar opened the **More** menu. It now opens your profile, which is what that block does in every app you already use.

## 🔎 Things that were quietly wrong

- **The update bar was invisible.** The strip that appears while a new version downloads was almost exactly the colour of the page behind it. It now reads as a bar.
- **The monthly attendance title was lower-case** — `agosto 2026` — and nothing on that page was spaced apart from anything else.
- **"What's new" was in English** even with the app in Italian. The two most recent releases now read in your language; older entries stay in English for now.
- **A missing space** before the version at the bottom of the More page.
- **A "view public profile" button that did nothing.** Public profiles need the community features, which this edition does not include, so the button is simply gone rather than there and unresponsive.
