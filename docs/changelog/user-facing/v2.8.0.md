# v2.8.0 — 2026-05-12

A focused follow-up to v2.7.0: the community feed is now **first-class for academy owners too**. Owners can post events, moderate the feed, and the notification fanout finally reaches them. Plus one visible bug fix on the notification toggles.

## ✨ Owners now have the community feed in their sidebar

A new **Community** entry lands in the dashboard sidebar between **Stats** and **Profile** (with the chat-bubbles icon). Tap it and you arrive on the same feed your athletes see — belt-promotion celebrations, owner-posted events, comments, RSVPs.

You can do everything an athlete can:
- 👏 **Clap** / 🙏 **Pray** reactions
- Write and delete your own comments
- Yes / Maybe / No RSVPs on event posts

The athlete-portal feed under `/dashboard/me/feed` is unchanged.

## ✨ "Post event" composer

Right above the feed, owners now see a **Post event** button. Tap it and a dialog opens with five fields:

- **Title** (required, 120 chars)
- **When** — a calendar with time picker (24h on EN, formato 24h su IT)
- **Where** (optional)
- **Details** (optional, up to 2000 chars)
- **Max attendees** (optional, leave empty for no cap)

Hit **Post event** and the new event card lands at the top of the feed immediately. Every athlete in your academy receives an inbox notification (default-on, opt-out lives on `/dashboard/profile` → Notifications).

V1 ships **create only** — editing or cancelling an event is V2. Plan accordingly until then.

## ✨ Owner moderation — delete posts and others' comments

A new trash icon appears on:

- Every post header on the feed (visible only to owners — athletes don't see it).
- Every comment in every thread, regardless of who wrote it.

Tap the trash and you get a confirmation dialog with a red **Delete** button (no accidents). On confirm the post / comment is removed from the feed for everyone, and from your local view immediately.

This was already the server-side rule (owners have always been authorized to moderate their academy) — the dashboard just hadn't surfaced the affordance until now.

## ✨ Owners now receive the event inbox notification

A small recipient-set fix: until v2.8.0 the **community_event_new** inbox notification only reached athletes with a linked user account. Owners who weren't the editor of the event were silently skipped (a vestige of the "the owner always IS the editor" assumption).

That's fixed now — the recipient set is *every academy user except the editor*, which means in the multi-owner future you're planning, every owner reads the inbox row about an event their co-owner posted. Today, owners still don't get notified about events they posted themselves (that's still the correct exclusion: the editor sees the post they just made appear in their own feed).

## 🐛 Notification toggles — green track + white handle, no more split colour

The toggle switches on `/dashboard/profile` → Notifications were rendering with a **half-purple, half-green** split visible on iOS Safari: the track flipped to green correctly when on, but the round knob stayed full indigo from the Material preset's default. iOS toggles use a white knob on a green track regardless of system theme — that's the shape you'll see now, in light and dark mode.
