# v2.41.0 — Budojo is now an app on your computer

The biggest change yet: Budojo now runs as an application installed on your own computer, instead of a website you log into. Everything you already use — athletes, documents, attendance, payments, belts — works exactly the same, but now it lives on your machine.

## 💻 Budojo runs on your computer

Budojo is now a Windows application you install and open like any other program. Your gym's data stays on your own computer — it doesn't travel to a server on the internet, and you don't need a connection to use it. Open the app and it's there, fast, whether or not you're online.

## 🔒 You're signed in automatically

Because Budojo is now your own app on your own machine, it remembers you. Open it and you're already signed in — no typing your email and password every time.

## 💾 Automatic backups, and a one-click restore

Since your data now lives on your computer, protecting it matters. Budojo backs itself up automatically while it's open, and you can make a backup any time from the new **Data & backup** page. If something ever goes wrong, you can restore an earlier backup from the same page in a couple of clicks.

One important thing to know: **keep a copy of your backups somewhere other than this computer** — a USB stick, or a synced folder like OneDrive or Google Drive. A backup that only lives on the same machine won't help if that machine is lost. The Data & backup page has the details.

## 🔔 Reminders pop up on your desktop

When a medical certificate is about to expire, Budojo now lets you know with a normal desktop notification — the same kind of pop-up other apps on your computer use. Click it and Budojo opens straight to what needs your attention.
