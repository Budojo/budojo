Cookie Policy

What Budojo stores on your device, and why. Aligned with the Italian Garante guidelines of 10 June 2021 and Article 5(3) of the e-Privacy directive.

Italiano · English

1. The short version

Budojo sets no cookies and uses no tracking, profiling or advertising tags. The app keeps a few values in its own storage on the device. Each one is needed for the app to work or remembers a choice you made, and none of them leaves the device.

The Windows and Android apps show no consent banner: nothing they store is optional.

2. What the app stores

Mirrored from docs/legal/cookie-audit.md, the auditor-readable inventory in our repository. The two are kept in lock-step by hand; the markdown is the canonical source.

Key Where Purpose Device
budojoLang localStorage Your language (English or Italian). PC and phone
budojoTheme localStorage Your theme: light, dark or the system's. PC and phone
budojoSyncLedger localStorage Sync bookkeeping: the last version this device synced, by device and folder. Only with the sync on. PC and phone
budojoSyncNamed localStorage Sync bookkeeping: which files each kept version uses, by their content hash. Only with the sync on. PC and phone
documents.showCancelled localStorage Whether an athlete's documents list shows cancelled documents. PC and phone
auth_token localStorage The sign-in token, kept in the app's private storage. Phone
budojoRestoredBackup localStorage The backup the phone was last brought back from, so signing in again goes straight in. Phone
budojo.reminders.told localStorage Which reminders were already shown, so none shows twice. Phone
budojo-stale-chunk-reload-attempted sessionStorage Set only if the app has to reload itself after an update, so it never loops. Removed once the app has run normally for 30 seconds. PC and phone

On the PC the sign-in token is not in this storage: the app's main process holds it, encrypted with the Windows keychain. The app sets no HTTP cookies, and uses no IndexedDB and no service worker cache.

3. Third parties

The third parties the app contacts are listed at /sub-processors. The links the owner opens (a technique's video, WhatsApp) open in the device's browser or app: any cookie those sites set lives there, under their own policies, not in Budojo.
