Account deletion

How to remove Budojo and the academy's data from your devices and from your Google account.

Italiano · English

1. There is no Budojo account to close

Budojo is a Windows and Android app that runs on the academy's own devices. No account lives on a Budojo server, and Budojo receives none of the data. So deleting means deleting the data where it is: on the PC, on the phone, and in the academy's Google account if it was connected.

2. Before you delete

  • If there is any chance you will want the data back, take a backup first (Data & backup → Back up now) and keep it somewhere safe, with your recovery code.
  • To keep a readable copy, on the PC: Settings → Account → Download my data saves a ZIP file with the academy's data and documents.

3. On the PC

  1. If Google Drive is connected: in Data & backup, choose Disconnect under Google Drive backup. The copies stop; the backups already on Drive stay until you delete them (§ 5).
  2. Uninstall Budojo from Windows.
  3. Delete the folder %APPDATA%\Budojo\. Uninstalling does not touch it: it holds the database, the documents, the local backups, the logs and the keys.
  4. Delete the archives in the backup folder, if you chose one: a USB stick, a network drive, a synced folder. Uninstalling leaves them there.

4. On the phone

Uninstall Budojo. Its data lives only in the app's private storage, excluded from Android's cloud backup and from the transfer to a new phone, so no copy of it stays on Android's side.

5. In the academy's Google account

Only if the academy connected Google Drive:

  • Delete the Budojo folder in Google Drive: the backups and the sync files. Then empty Drive's trash.
  • Delete Budojo's hidden app data, where the keys are: in Google Drive's settings, under Manage apps.
  • Remove Budojo's access from the Google account's security settings.

6. What Budojo keeps

Nothing. Budojo operates no server and keeps no copy, log or backup of the academy's data. Update checks reach GitHub, which sees the device's IP address and never any academy data.

7. When an athlete asks to be erased

Athletes exercise their right to erasure (GDPR Art. 17) with the academy, the controller, not with Budojo.

  • Removing an athlete in the app takes them off the roster and deletes their documents. Their record and history (attendance, payments, belts) stay in the database.
  • Older backups keep the data until they rotate out: about two weeks of the app being open.

8. Contact

  • Who controls the data — see the Privacy Policy (English) or the Informativa sulla Privacy (Italian, legal source of truth).
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the Italian DPA — garanteprivacy.it .
