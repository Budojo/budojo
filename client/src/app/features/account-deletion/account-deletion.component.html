Account deletion

How to request permanent deletion of your Budojo account and the data we hold about you, what is deleted, and what is retained.

Italiano · English

1. About Budojo

Budojo — website budojo.it — is a web application that helps martial-arts academies manage their roster, attendance, documents, payments, and community feed. This page describes how to request the permanent deletion of your Budojo account and the personal data associated with it, in accordance with Article 17 of EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR).

2. How to request deletion

Today — by email

Write to privacy@budojo.it from the email address you registered with. Subject line "Account deletion". Include your registered name so we can match the request to a single account. We confirm receipt within five working days and initiate the deletion on your behalf. The 30-day grace window (see § 5) starts at confirmation.

Coming soon — from the app

A "Delete account" entry will land in your profile page in a follow-up release. The in-app flow re-authenticates with your current password and goes through the same 30-day grace window. This UI is not yet shipped; the email path above is the canonical request channel today.

3. What is deleted

Once the grace window elapses, an hourly background job runs a hard-delete cascade. The following is permanently removed from the database and from file storage:

  • User account — first name, last name, display handle, email, phone, postal address, date of birth, avatar photo, password hash, API tokens, two-factor secret and recovery codes, active sessions, push notification subscriptions, notification and language preferences, login history rows.
  • Academy — if you are the registered owner: academy name, logo, address, contact phone, email, URLs.
  • Athletes — every athlete belonging to your academy, including any previously archived athletes: anagrafica, profile photos, every uploaded document (medical certificates, federation cards, ID scans), attendance history, payment history, belt promotion history.
  • Community feed — posts, comments, RSVPs, and reactions authored under your account.

Binary files on disk (logos, avatars, athlete photos, documents) are wiped after the database cascade commits, so the database state and the file system stay consistent.

4. What is retained

The following residual data is kept for a strictly limited time:

  • Application logs — at most 30 days, for fraud and abuse review.
  • CDN access logs (Cloudflare) — at most 30 days, same purpose.
  • Database backups — at most 30 days from the deletion, on automatic rotation.
  • Outbound email archives at the email provider — at most 30 days.

None of the above is exposed in the application; none is used to reconstruct an account or contact you after deletion. No personally identifiable data is retained beyond these windows.

5. Grace window — 30 days

When you make the request, Budojo records the date and schedules the actual deletion 30 days later. During this window:

  • You can cancel the deletion at any time by clicking the link in the deletion confirmation email (token-bound public link, no login required from the click) or by visiting your dashboard, where a banner offers a one-click cancellation.
  • Your account stays fully functional — login, dashboard access, and authenticated API calls all still work. The banner is the only visible signal.
  • Re-clicking the delete button while a request is already pending is a no-op — the scheduled date is not extended.

After the 30 days elapse, an hourly background job runs the cascade. Once that runs, the deletion is irreversible.

6. Export your data before deletion

Budojo exposes a one-click JSON export of your personal data through the authenticated GET /api/v1/me/export endpoint. The dashboard surfaces this as an "Export my data" button — exercise of your GDPR Art. 15 right of access and Art. 20 right of portability. We recommend running an export before confirming a deletion, because the data is unrecoverable after the grace window elapses.
